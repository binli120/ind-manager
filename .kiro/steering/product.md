# Product Overview

## IND-Manager-V2 (Document Authoring Platform)

A collaborative document authoring and management platform designed for pharmaceutical IND (Investigational New Drug) submissions. The application enables teams to create, edit, review, and submit regulatory documents with AI-powered gap analysis and compliance checking.

## Core Features

- **Project Management**: Multi-project workspace for managing drug submissions with sponsor and team assignment
- **Document Authoring**: Rich text editor (Tiptap) with real-time collaboration, section locking, and version control
- **Team Collaboration**: Role-based access control, team management, and multi-user document editing
- **Gap Analysis**: AI-powered compliance checking and readiness assessment for regulatory submissions
- **Review Center**: Comment threads, document review workflows, and approval processes
- **IND Submission**: Structured eCTD document management and submission tracking
- **Calendar & Timeline**: Submission deadlines and milestone tracking

## Target Users

Pharmaceutical companies, regulatory affairs teams, and document authors working on FDA IND submissions.
